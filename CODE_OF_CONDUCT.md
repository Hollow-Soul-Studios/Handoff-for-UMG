# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that address applies to this repository.

In short:

* Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.
* Keep issues on topic and free of advertising.
* Do not post confidential designs, credentials, license keys or purchase details.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat
offenders.

To report a problem privately, use the publisher contact on the Fab product page. Reports are handled privately.
